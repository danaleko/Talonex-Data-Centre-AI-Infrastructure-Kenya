Architecture & Engineering

Campus design concept

Modular, high-density infrastructure engineered specifically for modern compute and accelerator clusters.

Critical power

A/B distribution ensuring continuous, isolated delivery paths to all tenant compute halls with concurrent maintainability.

IT and GPU data halls

150 MW IT load across 3 × 50 MW modular expansion phases.

Connectivity

Diverse fibre and carrier meet-me rooms ensuring redundant carrier interconnects.

Liquid cooling

Direct-to-chip or rear-door, precision-matched to tenant equipment.

AI-ready infrastructure

Mixed rack densities, with cooling interfaces matched to tenant equipment.

Sustainable Energy Architecture

Power and energy concept

Reliability and operational readiness
