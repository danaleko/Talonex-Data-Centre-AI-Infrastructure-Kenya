Project Identity

Who we are

Talonex is the project company (SPV) for a proposed 150 MW green datacentre and AI infrastructure campus in Kenya. It is intended to hold the project rights, financing arrangements and operating contracts.

Project Status Notice: Final site rights and utility arrangements remain subject to completion. Capacity is developed in phases and released as customer demand is contracted.
Core Mission & Delivery Principle

Our purpose and approach

Our Purpose

To provide scalable infrastructure that helps customers host critical data and deploy demanding compute workloads in Kenya.

Our Approach

We develop capacity in stages, with power, cooling and connectivity planned together around customer requirements.

Corporate & Delivery Framework

Our development structure

Clear entity allocation establishing accountability, asset custody, and project governance.

Talonex

The project company, intended to hold the datacentre project rights, financing arrangements and operating contracts.

Barwasim Africa Consulting Ltd

The project proponent and development originator, coordinating the project’s development pathway.

A partnership-based delivery model

Specialist investors, engineering advisers, technology providers and operators will take part through defined mandates and project agreements. Governance, decision rights and responsibilities will follow the final project and investment agreements.

Phased Methodology

How we develop the project

A disciplined, demand-led lifecycle transitioning from feasibility study to operational capacity.

Step What it delivers
Site and feasibility Land and utility terms, environmental work and a defined Phase 1 scope
Engineering and customer commitments Detailed design, anchor specifications and commercial agreements
Investment and construction Approved financing, procurement packages and construction oversight
Commissioning and expansion Integrated tests, customer acceptance and demand-led capacity release
Expansion & Scaling Notice: An initial proof-of-concept hall will inform expansion. Later phases depend on contracted demand, utility capacity and funding.
ESG & Impact Commitments

Responsible development

Talonex intends to build environmental and social considerations into project design and delivery.

Resource efficiency

We will evaluate energy performance, water demand and cooling options, and track renewable energy and battery lifecycle requirements.

Environmental and social safeguards

We will complete site-specific assessments, stakeholder engagement and permit planning before construction commitments.

Local participation

We will focus on suitable procurement opportunities, workforce training and skills transfer through delivery partners.
