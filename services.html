Sustainable Energy Architecture

Power and energy concept

Engine-free green power combining dedicated solar PV generation, comprehensive BESS battery storage, and dual high-voltage grid paths.

Annual PUE target

Equivalent to 195 MW facility demand at full IT load under the design assumption.

  • Two separate grid supply paths: Feeding A and B critical power distribution networks independently.
  • Engine-free concept: An hourly dispatch study will confirm firm supply, charging arrangements and protected battery reserves.
Thermal Engineering

Cooling

Heat moves from the compute equipment to an outdoor rejection system through separate IT coolant and facility loops. The closed-loop design aims to limit make-up water.

Cooling selection will follow rack density, hourly climate and water availability.
